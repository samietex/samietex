## 👋 About me

I turn raw data into decisions people can trust. I've spent **5+ years** in data, and I like owning the whole path: the pipelines that bring data in, the KPIs and statistics that make sense of it, and the dashboards and recommendations people act on.

At Syno International, a market research company, I work on brand tracking and pricing studies for international clients: significance testing so teams stop reacting to noise, measuring what advertising and sponsorship do for a brand, and AWS pipelines that stop and alert on bad data instead of publishing it. When a number looks wrong, I find out why.

I also teach and write: **18+ tutorials** for DataCamp, **40+ students** taught, and a newsletter, [All About Data & More](https://aaboutdata.substack.com), that helps people become job-ready in data.
